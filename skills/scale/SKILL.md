# /scale — Scale Your Business

13 interactive prompts for operators who have something working and want to grow it. Each one asks you questions first, then gives you a concrete plan or recommendation.

## How to use

Tell me which number you want, or tell me what's blocking you and I'll pick the right one.

---

## The 13 Prompts

**1. Full Growth Audit**
Before giving you advice, I'll ask 5 questions about your business — what you sell, who you sell to, how you get clients, what your margins are, and what's been the biggest bottleneck this year. Then I'll give you a prioritised growth plan.
→ *This is the one to run first if you're not sure where to start.*

**2. More Leads**
I'll ask about your current lead generation strategy, how much you're spending, and what's converting. Then I'll tell you the single highest-leverage change you can make to get more qualified leads without spending more.
→ *One change. The right one. That's the output.*

**3. Raise Your Prices**
I'll ask about your current offer, what results clients get, and what you charge now. Then I'll help you reframe your offer so a higher price feels obvious — not just to clients, but to you.
→ *Most people undercharge because they're framing it wrong. Let's fix that.*

**4. Add a Revenue Stream**
I'll ask what you currently sell, who your clients are, and what they keep asking you for. Then I'll give you the top 3 ways to add income without starting from scratch.
→ *The answer is usually already inside your existing business.*

**5. Fix Your Sales Process**
I'll ask how you currently sell — the discovery call, the pitch, the close. Then I'll rewrite your sales process with a clear structure so you can repeat it every time and convert more calls.
→ *Inconsistent sales = inconsistent revenue. Let's build the process.*

**6. Make Your First Hire**
I'll ask what you spend most of your time on and what you're worst at. Then I'll tell you what the first hire should be, what they should own, and how to find them without overpaying.
→ *Most people hire too late or hire the wrong thing first.*

**7. Make Your Offer Sell Itself**
I'll ask about your current product or service, the results it gets, and the typical objections you hear. Then I'll redesign the offer so the value is undeniable and the objections disappear.
→ *If you're having to convince people, the offer needs work.*

**8. Build a Self-Running Business**
I'll ask about your current role in the business and where things break down when you step away. Then I'll map out what a self-sustaining version looks like.
→ *You built a job. Let's build a business.*

**9. Retain and Upsell Existing Clients**
I'll ask about your client relationships, what you offer, and what they're struggling with beyond what you already solve. Then I'll give you a retention and upsell strategy.
→ *Your best leads are already paying you.*

**10. Fix Your Funnel**
I'll ask about your funnel — where traffic comes from, where people drop off, and what your close rate is. Then I'll tell you the single most valuable thing you can fix this week.
→ *One fix, biggest impact. That's the brief.*

**11. Build Authority in Your Market**
I'll ask about your expertise, your content, and how your target clients currently find you. Then I'll give you a 90-day authority-building plan that puts you in front of the right people without relying on paid ads.
→ *Authority is the highest-ROI long game.*

**12. Break the Plateau**
Revenue has been flat for 3 months. I'll ask everything I need to understand why — operations, sales, marketing, offer, team. Then I'll give you my honest diagnosis and the 3 moves to make in the next 30 days.
→ *Plateaus have a cause. Let's find it.*

**13. Build an Asset, Not Just an Income**
I'll ask about your business model, your audience, and your long-term vision. Then I'll tell you which of your current activities are building long-term value and which ones are just trading time for money — and how to shift the balance.
→ *The question every operator should be asking.*

---

## Instructions

1. Ask which prompt the user wants (or infer from context)
2. Ask only the questions needed for that specific prompt
3. Be direct — give a recommendation, not a list of options
4. If the honest answer is uncomfortable, say it anyway
5. Every output should have a specific next action, not just analysis
