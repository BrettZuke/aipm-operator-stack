# /first-business — Build Your First Business

12 interactive prompts for going from "I want to build something" to "I'm ready to launch." Each one asks you questions first, then gives you something concrete.

## How to use

Tell me which number you want, or tell me where you're stuck and I'll pick the right one.

---

## The 12 Prompts

**1. Find Your Business Idea**
I'll ask you 5 questions about your skills, experience, and what you've helped people with in your life — personal or professional. Then I'll give you the top 3 business ideas that would be the most natural fit for you.
→ *Answer honestly. The best idea is usually hiding in what you already do.*

**2. Find Your Offer**
Based on what you know about your skill or topic, what would be the most valuable thing you could teach or offer to someone who's 6 months behind you? I'll give you 3 specific product or service ideas with a rough price point for each.
→ *Tell me your skill/expertise. I'll build 3 offer options.*

**3. Find Your Niche**
I'll ask questions to help you get specific about your target market — then tell you which sub-niche has the most demand and least competition right now.
→ *Tell me broadly who you want to work with. I'll help you get specific.*

**4. AI-Proof Your Offer**
I'll ask about what you do and what your clients actually pay for. Then I'll tell you which part of your offer cannot be replaced by AI — and how to position that as your main value.
→ *The answer to this is your real moat. Let's find it.*

**5. Understand Your Ideal Client**
What does your ideal client's day look like? I'll walk you through their day, what frustrates them, what they're searching for online, and what would make them say yes immediately.
→ *Tell me who you want to serve. I'll map their world.*

**6. Define Your End Goal**
Not just the revenue number. I'll ask about your lifestyle, your freedom, your time, and what you actually want your days to look like — then reverse-engineer what kind of business gets you there.
→ *Most people skip this. Don't.*

**7. Build Your Product Suite**
Based on your expertise, I'll give you 3 digital product ideas — one under $50, one under $200, one over $500. I'll tell you which one to launch first and why.
→ *Tell me your topic/expertise. I'll design the ladder.*

**8. Make Your First $5K**
What's the fastest way for someone with your background to make their first $5,000 online? I won't give you a vague answer — I'll give you a specific path based on what you know and what you can offer.
→ *Tell me your skills and situation. I'll give you the shortest path.*

**9. Add AI to Your Offer**
How could you add AI into your offer in a way that genuinely makes it more valuable to your clients — without it feeling forced or fake?
→ *Tell me what you currently offer. I'll find the real AI angle.*

**10. Write Your Positioning Statement**
I work with [type of client] to help them [achieve outcome]. I'm different because [differentiator]. Let's punch that up — make it confident, clear, and like something you'd actually say out loud.
→ *Give me your rough version. I'll sharpen it.*

**11. Avoid the Big Mistake**
What's the one thing you should NOT do when starting this business? Based on what you've told me, I'll tell you the biggest mistake you're likely to make and how to avoid it.
→ *Tell me about your situation. I'll be honest with you.*

**12. Launch Readiness Check**
Are you actually ready to launch, or is there one thing you need to fix first? I'll ask about your offer, your audience, and what's holding you back — then give you a straight answer.
→ *No more waiting. Let's find out if you're ready.*

---

## Instructions

1. Ask which prompt the user wants (or infer from context)
2. Ask the questions relevant to that prompt — no more, no less
3. Be direct and specific — no vague frameworks, no "it depends" non-answers
4. If they're clearly not ready for something, tell them
