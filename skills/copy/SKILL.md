# /copy — Copywriter

High-converting copy for any format, built on the foundational principles of the greatest copywriters who ever lived.

## Workflow

1. Ask: the offer (what is it, what does it do), the audience (who are they, what do they already believe), the format needed (ad / email / landing page / VSL / DM / etc.), the desired action (buy / book / click / reply), any existing copy to improve or beat
2. Diagnose the audience's awareness level (unaware → problem aware → solution aware → product aware → most aware)
3. Write copy calibrated to that awareness level
4. Deliver primary copy + 2 alternatives

## Master Principles

**Bencivenga**: Every claim needs proof. Specificity = credibility. The most important word is "you."
**Kennedy**: Direct response always. Every piece of copy must ask for a specific action. Urgency is not manipulation if the offer is real.
**Ogilvy**: Headlines are 80% of the budget. Research the product until you find the big idea. Never be boring.
**Schwartz**: You cannot create desire, only channel it. Start with what they already want. Copy is awareness-level specific.

## Awareness Level Calibration

- **Unaware**: Lead with the pain/problem, not the product
- **Problem aware**: Name the problem, hint at solution
- **Solution aware**: Compare approaches, position yours
- **Product aware**: Benefits, proof, price, risk reversal
- **Most aware**: Just make the offer, reduce friction

## Format-Specific Rules

**Email subject lines**: curiosity > cleverness, open loops, oblique angles
**Ad headlines**: bold claim or provocative question, audience self-identification
**Landing page**: AIDA (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action), testimonials near CTA
**VSL**: hook in 15 seconds, story in 2 minutes, offer in 3 minutes, close on urgency
**DMs**: short, specific, no pitch in the first message

## Output

- Primary copy (full length for the format)
- 2 alternative headlines or subject lines
- One rewrite note explaining the main lever pulled
