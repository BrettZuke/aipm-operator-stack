# Where these skills came from

Most of the skills in this repo were written for the AI Partner Method. Some came from
open source projects, and those keep their original authors' names and licences.

## Included in this repo

| Skills | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| 47 marketing skills (`ads`, `cold-email`, `cro`, `lead-magnets`, `offers`, `pricing`, `prospecting`, `referrals`, `schema`, `site-architecture` and more) | [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) | MIT, Copyright (c) 2025 Corey Haines |
| `hallmark` | [Nutlope/hallmark](https://github.com/Nutlope/hallmark) | MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Hallmark contributors |
| `planning-with-files` | [OthmanAdi/planning-with-files](https://github.com/OthmanAdi/planning-with-files) | MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Ahmad Adi |
| 14 engineering skills (`brainstorming`, `writing-plans`, `test-driven-development`, `systematic-debugging`, `verification-before-completion` and more) | [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) | MIT |

The MIT licence text below covers all four.

## Not included, and it cannot be

**[anthropics/skills](https://github.com/anthropics/skills)** is Anthropic's own set, and
it is genuinely useful. `docx`, `pdf`, `pptx` and `xlsx` let Claude produce real client
deliverables instead of markdown you then reformat by hand.

**Those skills are proprietary.** Each one ships a `LICENSE.txt` stating that users may
not reproduce, copy, redistribute, sublicense or transfer them to a third party. So they
are not in this repo and never will be. Anyone handing you a copy of them inside their
own product is breaking those terms.

`./install-extra-skills.sh` fetches them from Anthropic's own public repository onto your
machine, for your own use with Claude Code. It shows you the licence before it does
anything. Read the terms and decide for yourself. Nothing else here depends on it.
